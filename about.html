We preserve the visual memory of St. Sylvester's College while giving students a space to see, create and tell stories.

OUR PURPOSE

Observe. Create.
Remember.

SSCPC is the photography community of St. Sylvester's College, Kandy. From school events and sporting moments to everyday campus life, our members turn fleeting moments into a lasting visual archive.

We encourage students to develop technical photography skills, visual awareness, editing discipline and the confidence to tell stories through images.
